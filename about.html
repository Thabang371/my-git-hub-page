A Century of Excellence

Discover the foundational milestones behind the world's most historic automotive manufacturer.

1886: The Invention

The history of Mercedes-Benz began when designer Carl Benz patented his three-wheeled Motorwagen. This historic milestone marked the birth of the modern automobile system, transitioning global society away from animal-drawn transport layouts forever.

1926: The Merger

The modern corporate structure emerged following a strategic merger between DMG (founded by Gottlieb Daimler) and Benz & Cie. This combined engineering powerhouse established the official trademark three-pointed star logo seen across our layouts today.

1954: Safety Innovations

Mercedes-Benz redefined product development standards by engineering crumple zones and impact-absorbing vehicle subframes. These structural design elements prioritized driver safety, proving that performance must balance responsibly with structural protection standards.

The Future: Sustainable Mobility

Moving forward, the historic brand transitions its entire production engineering experience towards zero-emission targets. Our modern history is currently being written through the advanced high-voltage architectures showcased on our Innovation tracks.
