Our History

ERA: 1886

The Birth of the Automobile

Karl Benz awards the world its first official automotive patent for a three-wheeled motor vehicle powered by a gas engine. This exact historical coordinate fundamentally redefines global land transportation modules permanently.

ERA: 1951

The Crumple Zone x

Béla Barényi introduces the structural crumple zone architectural pattern into mainstream vehicle development. By engineering safe, deformable structures around rigid safety passenger cells, cabin impact survival coordinates improve exponentially.

ERA: 2026

The Sustainable Luxury Transition

Deploying highly efficient digital aerodynamic matrices globally, the brand scales zero-emission electric luxury fleets. The design framework balances carbon neutrality timelines alongside raw performance driving dynamics.
