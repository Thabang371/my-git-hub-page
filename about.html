Brand Heritage & Profiles


A comprehensive timeline view of pioneering engineering benchmarks that defined global automotive luxury.

1886: The Invention Horizon

The foundational history of Mercedes-Benz was set in motion when Carl Benz patented his three-wheeled Motorwagen structure.

This historic mechanical blueprint marked the official birthday of the modern automobile system, pivoting logistics away from animal-drawn carts entirely.

1926: Strategic Corporate Merger

To navigate global industrial pressures, DMG (founded by Gottlieb Daimler) and Benz & Cie legally unified corporate architectures.

This high-profile integration consolidated production lines and introduced the classic three-pointed corporate emblem used across our modern layouts today.
