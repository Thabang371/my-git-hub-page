Our History

A timeline tracking over a century of automotive paradigm definition and luxury engineering.

ERA: 1886

The Birth of the Automobile

Karl Benz is awarded the world's first official automotive patent for a three-wheeled motor vehicle powered by a gas engine — permanently redefining global land transportation.

ERA: 1951

The Crumple Zone

Béla Barényi introduces the structural crumple zone into mainstream vehicle development — engineering deformable structures around a rigid passenger cell, dramatically improving cabin survival in a collision.

ERA: 2026

The Sustainable Luxury Transition

Deploying highly efficient digital aerodynamics globally, the brand scales zero-emission electric luxury fleets — balancing carbon-neutrality timelines with raw performance.
